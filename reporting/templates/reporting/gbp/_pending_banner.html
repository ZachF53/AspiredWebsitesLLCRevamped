⏳ Awaiting Google API approval

Google requires manual approval for any project to call the Business Profile APIs above 0 requests per minute. Until that's granted, the dashboards on this page will be empty and the bind/sync flows will return 429 Too Many Requests.

What to do: Apply for higher quota in your Cloud Console (APIs & Services → Quotas), search the My Business APIs, and request 60 RPM or higher. Google typically reviews in 1–4 weeks. Once approved, no code changes needed — the existing dashboards start populating.

Meanwhile the OAuth connect flow still works (you can pre-bind tokens) and the comp tier mechanism lets you offer GBP in your Growth/Dominant tiers as soon as access lands.
