{# ───────── Why it matters for HVAC ───────── #}
Why It Matters

Reviews Are How HVAC Customers Choose

A homeowner searching for emergency AC repair picks from what Google shows them. Review count and recency decide who gets the call. The same is true for plumbing, electrical and any trade where the homeowner picks from the map.

Asking Doesn’t Scale

A busy technician isn’t going to remember to ask for a review on every job, every time. Automating the ask means it happens consistently instead of depending on someone remembering.

Recent Reviews Carry More Weight

Forty five-star reviews from three years ago read as stale. A steady stream of recent reviews signals a business that’s active right now.

Compliance

What This Is And Isn’t

What It Does

  • Asks every customer the same way after every completed job
  • Sends the request while the work is still fresh
  • Runs without anyone on your team remembering to do it

What It Doesn’t Do

  • Filter for happy customers only or hide unhappy ones
  • Offer a discount or incentive for a review
  • Write or post reviews on a customer’s behalf

Asking every customer the same way, without incentives, is what keeps this inside Google’s policies. Selective or paid-for review requests are what get accounts penalized.
