Why It Matters

Reviews Are How HVAC Customers Actually Choose

A homeowner searching for emergency AC repair picks from what Google shows them. Review count and recency decide who gets the call. The same is true for plumbing, electrical and any trade where the homeowner picks from the map.

Straight Answers

What This Is And Isn’t

What It Does

  • Asks every customer the same way after every completed job
  • Sends the request while the work is still fresh
  • Runs without anyone on your team remembering to do it

What It Doesn’t Do

  • Filter for happy customers only or hide unhappy ones
  • Offer a discount or incentive for a review
  • Write or post reviews on a customer’s behalf

Asking every customer the same way, without incentives, is what keeps this inside Google’s policies. Selective or paid-for review requests are what actually get accounts penalized.

Common Questions

Review Automation, Answered

How does it work?

When a job is marked complete, the system automatically sends the customer a request to leave a Google review. No one has to remember to ask.

Can I control the timing?

Yes. The request goes out after the job is marked complete, while the work is still fresh in the customer’s mind.

Does this risk violating Google’s policies?

No. The system asks every customer the same way after a real completed job, with no incentive and no filtering for positive reviews only. That consistency is what keeps it compliant.

Which job systems does it connect to?

It depends on what you already run. Tell us your setup on the call and we’ll confirm how the request gets triggered before you sign.
