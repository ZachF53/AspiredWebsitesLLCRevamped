What We Check

Four Scores That Decide Whether Your Website Gets Calls

Performance

How fast your page loads on a phone. Slow sites lose visitors before they ever see your phone number, and Google ranks slow pages lower.

SEO

The basics search engines read: page titles, meta descriptions, crawlable links and mobile-friendly text. Miss these and good pages stay hard to find.

Accessibility

Color contrast, image alt text, form labels and buttons a screen reader can use. It affects real customers and carries legal risk under the ADA.

Best Practices

HTTPS on every request, no browser errors, no outdated libraries with known security holes. The closest public measure of how carefully a site was built.
