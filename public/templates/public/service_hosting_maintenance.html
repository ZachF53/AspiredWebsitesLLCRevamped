What You Get

What “Managed” Actually Means Here

Your Own Server

Every client site runs on its own server — never shared hosting, never another business’s traffic slowing yours down or their compromise becoming your problem.

Patched, Not Parked

The server, its operating system, and your site’s dependencies get security updates as they’re needed, applied by a CISSP-certified engineer, not left for an outage to reveal.

SSL That Never Lapses

Certificate renewal is handled for you. The padlock stays on, and visitors never hit a browser warning where your website should be.

Uptime, Watched

Your site is monitored around the clock against a 99.9% uptime target. It’s a target, not a guarantee — and the monthly report shows you the actual number.

A Monthly Security Report

On the first of each month: a one-page summary of an automated scan — dependency vulnerabilities, file integrity, SSL status, security headers, and uptime. If something needs attention, fixing it is covered by your plan.

The Engineer, Not A Queue

Questions and problems go to the person who runs your server. We answer within 24 hours, seven days a week.

Common Questions

Hosting, Answered

Am I locked in?

No. Both plans are month-to-month with 30 days’ notice. Once your build is paid off, the files are yours — leaving is a support ticket, not a dispute.

What happens if I cancel?

Once the build is paid off, the site keeps working; the plan buys updates, monitoring, and support, not the right to keep your own website online. Standard code on standard hosting means any competent developer can take it over.

Who actually does the work?

The engineer who built the platform. See about for the credentials and how to verify them.
