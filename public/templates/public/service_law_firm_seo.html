Why Most Attorney SEO Fails

You’ve Probably Paid For SEO Before. Here’s Why It Didn’t Work.

Legal is one of the most competitive verticals in search. It is also one of the most heavily marketed-to, which means most firms have already been burned once.

You Ranked For Words Nobody Searches

A report showing you at #1 for “experienced litigation counsel metro area” is worthless if twelve people a year type it. Real law firm SEO starts with what your prospects actually search — usually something far blunter, like “car accident lawyer near me.”

Impressions went up. Traffic went up. Did the phone ring with cases you actually wanted? If your reporting never answered that, you were being measured on the wrong thing.

{# ───────── Practice area architecture ───────── #}
The Structural Fix

Why Practice-Area Pages Decide Everything

If you change one thing about your firm’s site this year, make it this.

A page can rank for one intent. A prospect searching “divorce lawyer” and a prospect searching “DUI attorney” are two different people with two different problems — and one combined Practice Areas page cannot be the best answer for both. Google will pick a competitor who wrote a whole page about the one thing.

What This Looks Like In Practice

Each area of law gets its own URL and its own substantive page: what the process looks like, what it costs, how long it takes, what makes your firm the right call for that specific problem. Internally linked so the strongest pages lift the newer ones.

Why It Compounds

Each page is a separate entry point. A firm with six real practice-area pages has six chances to be found instead of one. That is also why it takes months rather than weeks — you are building an asset, not flipping a switch.

Building the pages is web design work; ranking them is SEO work. If your site can’t support this structure, start with custom law firm web design instead — then layer SEO on a foundation that can actually hold it.
