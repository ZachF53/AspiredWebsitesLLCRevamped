  • At signing: the build payment (in full, or the first of 24 monthly installments) and the first month of the Full Plan or Hosting + Security, if you chose one.

Ownership & Fine Print

What You Own, and What the Terms Actually Say

The most expensive thing about a cheap website is usually not being able to leave it. Here is both halves of that, in plain English.

What you own once paid off, and what happens if you leave
  • The source code. Every file. Yours to take, host anywhere, or hand to another developer.
  • The content. Copy, images, structure, including anything we wrote for you.
  • The domain. Registered in your name from day one. We never hold a client’s domain.
  • The data. Form submissions and reviews go to you, not into a platform you rent access to.
  • The site keeps working if you leave. Once the build is paid off, cancelling the Full Plan stops updates and support, not your website. Files and database are handed over free.
  • No lock-in. Month-to-month after the build term, no annual contract, no exit fee. Leaving is a support ticket, not a dispute.
Read the fine print
  • {{ GUARANTEE_DAYS }}-day guarantee. The window starts the day you sign. If you cancel within it, we refund {{ GUARANTEE_REFUND_PERCENT }}% of everything you’ve paid, keep {{ GUARANTEE_RETAINED_PERCENT }}% for the work already done, and cancel any remaining payments. After {{ GUARANTEE_DAYS }} days our fix-it commitment applies: anything we built that doesn’t work as agreed gets fixed at no charge.
  • Signed up online for a plan only? Without a services agreement, your first month is refunded in full if you ask within 30 days of the first charge.
  • Ownership on the installment. We hold ownership of the site until the 24 payments are done; it’s live and working for you the whole time. If payments stop, the site stays up while we sort it out. We’ll always contact you before anything is suspended (14 days’ notice).
  • If you go quiet. If a build stalls more than 30 days because we can’t get feedback, restarting it costs 25% of the build price. We’d rather never charge it; just tell us if life gets busy.
  • Revisions. Two rounds are included. More is {{ hourly_display }}, quoted first.
  • Uptime. We target 99.9% on DigitalOcean. It’s a target, not a guarantee, and outages caused by third parties or DNS are outside it.
  • Liability. Capped at what you paid us in the previous three months. Georgia law governs.
