How billing works

  • At signing: the build payment (in full, or the first of 24 monthly installments) and the first month of the Full Plan or Hosting + Security, if you chose one.
  • Before launch: we set up your dedicated server the day you pay, and your site is built and hosted on it from the start, so the plan is working for you before the site goes live.
  • Live from day one: your own server, a staging site you can watch take shape, security monitoring, and a client portal login that shows exactly which build stage you’re in.
  • Installments: 24 monthly payments on the same date each month. After the 24th, the build is paid off and yours.
  • Full Plan and Hosting + Security: billed monthly, month-to-month, 30 days’ notice to cancel. Always billed monthly.
  • Anything out of scope: {{ hourly_display }}, quoted and approved before work starts, invoiced after it’s done.

Ownership

Your Website Should Belong to You

The most expensive thing about a cheap website is usually not being able to leave it.

What You Own Once Paid Off, and What Happens If You Leave

  • The source code. Every file. Yours to take, host anywhere, or hand to another developer.
  • The content. Copy, images, structure, including anything we wrote for you.
  • The domain. Registered in your name from day one. We never hold a client’s domain.
  • The data. Form submissions and reviews go to you, not into a platform you rent access to.
  • The site keeps working if you leave. Once the build is paid off, cancelling the Full Plan stops updates and support, not your website. Files and database are handed over free.
  • No lock-in. Month-to-month after the build term, no annual contract, no exit fee. Leaving is a support ticket, not a dispute.
