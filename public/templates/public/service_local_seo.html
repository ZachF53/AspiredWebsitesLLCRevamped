Why Local Is Different

The Map Pack Is The Whole Game

Local search doesn’t work like national search, and treating it the same is why most local SEO underdelivers.

When someone searches for a service near them, Google shows three businesses in a map block above the normal results. Most people pick one of those three. You can rank fourth organically and still be invisible — which is why local SEO is a different discipline, not a smaller version of the same one.

Proximity You Can’t Buy

Google weighs how close you are to the searcher. You can’t change your location, but you can make sure you show up for every area you genuinely serve — and stop competing for areas you don’t.

Consistency Beats Volume

Your name, address and phone number have to match everywhere they appear. One old suite number on a directory nobody reads still muddies the signal. Cleanup is unglamorous and it moves the needle.

Reviews Are Ranking And Conversion

Volume and recency affect visibility. They affect whether anyone calls you even more. A steady trickle of recent, detailed reviews beats forty five-star ratings from three years ago.

Your Profile Is A Landing Page

Categories, services, hours, photos, Q&A, posts. Most businesses fill in the name and address and stop. A fully-built profile outranks a half-built one from a bigger company more often than you’d think.

What We Do

The Five Parts Of Local Search Engine Optimization

Google Business Profile

Correct primary category, honest secondary categories, full service list, real photos, service areas, and Q&A seeded with questions customers actually ask. The single highest-leverage local asset you own.

Citation Cleanup

Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Facebook, Yelp, BBB, industry directories — one consistent listing each. Quality over quantity. No junk directory packages, which do nothing and sometimes hurt.

Review Workflow

A repeatable request after every successful job, so reviews arrive steadily instead of in one suspicious burst. Never incentivised, never conditioned on sentiment — both violate Google’s policies and readers can tell.

On-Site Local Signals

Consistent NAP in the footer, LocalBusiness schema, an accurate contact page, and genuinely useful location pages where the demand justifies one. Never thin, spun city pages — those get discounted.

Local Content

Pages answering what local customers actually search: what you charge, which areas you cover, how fast you can get there. Useful first, optimised second.

Reporting That Names Numbers

Map-pack positions, profile views, calls, direction requests, and inquiries from organic search. Monthly, in plain English, with what we’re changing next.

What Local SEO Can And Can’t Do

Local SEO is a crowded, heavily-sold market. Here is the version without the pitch.

What It Can Do

  • Get you into the map pack for the areas you genuinely serve
  • Move faster than national SEO — weeks, not quarters, for profile work
  • Turn your Google profile into a second front door
  • Build review flow that keeps working after you stop paying us
  • Cost less per customer than paid ads over time

What It Can’t Do

  • Guarantee a map-pack position. Nobody controls Google’s index
  • Overcome proximity. You will not consistently rank in a suburb 40 miles away
  • Fix a one-star reputation. We can’t bury real reviews and won’t try
  • Work at a fake address. Virtual offices get listings suspended
  • Replace answering your phone. The lead still has to reach a human

Common Questions

Local SEO, Answered

What is local SEO?

The work that gets your business shown when someone nearby searches for what you sell — especially in the map pack above the normal results. Proximity, your profile and listing consistency matter more here than raw content volume.

How long does it take?

Faster than national SEO. Profile fixes can shift map visibility in weeks; citations and reviews compound over two to three months. Competitive categories in big metros take longer.

Do I need a physical address?

Not necessarily. If you go to customers, configure Google Business Profile as a service-area business — address hidden, service areas declared. Never use a virtual office as a storefront; Google suspends listings for it.

Do reviews affect rankings?

Yes — volume and recency both. And they affect whether anyone calls even more. We set up a repeatable request after successful jobs, never incentivised and never conditioned on sentiment.

What does it cost?

Part of our monthly maintenance plans, $299–$1,199/month depending on scope. No setup fee, no annual contract. Full breakdown on pricing.

Can you work on a site you didn’t build?

Usually. We audit first. If the platform can’t output clean markup or you don’t own the code, we’ll tell you before you spend money the platform will waste.
