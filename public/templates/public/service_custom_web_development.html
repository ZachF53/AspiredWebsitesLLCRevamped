{# ───────── What custom actually means ───────── #}
Definitions

What “Custom” Actually Means

The word gets used loosely enough to be meaningless. Here’s the version that matters to you.

What It Isn’t

Buying a $59 theme and changing the colours. Installing a page builder and dragging blocks around. Both are legitimate ways to get a website — neither is custom development, and you should not pay custom prices for either.

What It Is

Every page structured around a decision someone made about your business. The markup, the styling and the behaviour written for this site and no other. Nothing loaded because a theme author assumed you might want it one day.

The practical test: can the site do the specific thing your business needs? A template can only do what it was built to do. If your answer is “we work around it,” you are paying for that workaround in conversions every month.

{# ───────── Custom vs WordPress ───────── #}
The Real Comparison

Custom Development vs WordPress

We build custom. We’ll still tell you when WordPress is the better answer.

WordPress Is The Better Choice If

  • You publish constantly and several people need to write
  • You need a specific plugin ecosystem — complex e-commerce, memberships, courses
  • Your team already knows it and switching costs more than it saves
  • You need it live in days rather than weeks

Custom Is The Better Choice If

  • Speed matters — no theme overhead, no plugin stack to load
  • You’re tired of updates, incompatibilities and things silently breaking
  • You want a smaller attack surface: no admin login, no plugin CVEs
  • The site has a specific job a generic template can’t do
  • You want to own the code rather than depend on a plugin author

The full version of this comparison, including the cost side, is in our insights.

{# ───────── Honest limits ───────── #}
Straight Answers

Where Custom Development Isn’t Worth It

We’d rather lose the project than sell you something you don’t need.

You need one page

Hours, phone number, a photo. A $16/month builder does that well and we’d be charging you for craftsmanship nobody will notice. Spend the difference on something that grows the business.

You need it next week

A proper build takes three to four weeks depending on package, and most of that is content and review rather than code. If the deadline is real, a template now beats custom later.

Your problem isn’t the website

If nobody can find you, a rebuild won’t fix that on its own — that’s SEO. If the site converts fine, leave it alone.

You want cheapest

We’re not the cheapest and won’t pretend to be. If price is the deciding factor, a freelancer on a template is a legitimate answer.

The site is written for your business rather than adapted from a theme. Structure, pages and functionality follow what the business needs, not what a template supports.

How long does it take?

Three weeks for an Essential build, four for Premium. Content is the stage that slips, so we draft it rather than waiting on you.

Can I update it myself?

Yes, for what genuinely changes — services, staff, posts. The rest stays static, because static pages can’t break and never need patching.
