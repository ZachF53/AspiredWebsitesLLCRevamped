What We Actually Do

The Four Layers Of SEO That Matter.

SEO isn't a single thing — it's four things working at the same time. We do all of them. Most agencies do one or two and pretend that's enough.

1. Technical SEO

Site speed, mobile usability, crawl budget, schema, sitemap, hreflang, canonical tags, Core Web Vitals. The foundation Google evaluates before it even looks at your content.

2. On-Page SEO

Titles, headings, meta descriptions, internal linking, content depth, keyword targeting that doesn't read like a robot wrote it. The work that turns one ranking page into ten.

3. Local SEO

Google Business Profile optimization, citation building, review acquisition, local landing pages by city. The work that makes you visible to a 5-mile radius of buyers.

4. Content & Authority

Strategic content that answers the questions your customers are actually asking. Internal linking that distributes ranking power. The slow-and-steady work that compounds over months.
