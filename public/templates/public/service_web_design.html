Your Current Website Is Costing You Business.

If you have a site, here’s what’s probably wrong with it. If you don’t have one yet, skip to Just starting out.

Loads Too Slowly

Google’s own mobile research found that as a page’s load time goes from one second to three, the chance the visitor gives up jumps by about a third — and it only gets worse from there. Speed isn't a nice-to-have. It's the entire game.

Built On Someone Else's Platform

GoDaddy site builders, Wix, Squarespace: you don't own that site. They do. The day you leave, the site disappears with them.

No Clear Call To Action

Visitors land, scroll, and leave. There's no obvious next step, no compelling reason to call, no clear path from "I'm interested" to "I'm a client." That's pure waste.

Looks Like Everyone Else's

A recycled template means a recycled first impression. Your prospects can tell the difference between a real site and a $39 theme, even if they can't articulate why.
