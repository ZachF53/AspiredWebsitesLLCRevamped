The Honest Version

Do You Actually Need A Custom Website?

Sometimes the answer is no. We’d rather say so than sell you something you don’t need.

A Builder Is Probably Fine If…

  • You need a single page with your hours and phone number
  • Nobody finds you through search and that’s okay
  • Your budget is genuinely a few hundred dollars
  • You’re testing whether the business works at all

Wix and Squarespace are good products. Use one, spend the money elsewhere, and come back when the site needs to do more.

Custom Pays For Itself If…

  • You compete on search and every competitor uses the same template
  • The site needs to take bookings, intake, or payments properly
  • One customer is worth more than the build cost
  • Speed matters — you’re losing mobile visitors before they see anything
  • You want to own it instead of renting it forever

Small Business Website Questions

How much does it cost?

$2,500–$4,500 depending on pages and features. Hosting $150/year. Maintenance optional from $299/month. No licence fee for the site — you own it.

Is custom worth it over Wix or Squarespace?

If you need a brochure page, a builder is fine and cheaper. Custom is worth it when the site has a job to do. We’ll tell you which situation you’re in.

How long does it take?

About six weeks. The stage that usually slips is content — so we draft it rather than waiting on you.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Code, content, domain, hosting. Leave and take every file — the site keeps working.

What if I stop paying maintenance?

The site keeps working. Maintenance buys updates, monitoring, backups and support — not the right to keep your own site online.

Yes — that’s a slightly different job. See website redesign.
