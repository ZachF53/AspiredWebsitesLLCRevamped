Why It Matters

The Call You Miss Goes to Whoever Picks Up

No heat, 9pm, January

That caller is not leaving a voicemail and waiting until Tuesday. They are dialling the next three numbers on Google until somebody answers. That job was worth anywhere from three hundred dollars to a full system.

You are already busy

You cannot answer the phone with your hands inside a condenser, and the office is closed at six. The receptionist does not need a lunch break, does not call in sick, and does not mind the fourth call about the same thermostat.

Half your calls are junk

Extended warranties, solar, SEO cold calls. If the receptionist answers first, you never hear them. Only real customers reach your phone, which is the part owners tell us they notice in week one.

What It Is, and What It Is Not

What it is

  • A receptionist that answers from your notes, not from guesswork
