Bugs found by this run, fixed and re-verified (commit `a8332cd`):
1. Schedule-created subscriptions are "active" while their first invoice is still a draft, so installment agreements were marked paid with **nothing charged**. Checkout now finalizes and charges that invoice.
2. With no ledger rows, the guarantee "completed" a **$0 refund** and cancelled everything. It now refuses.
3. Receipt PDF crashed for invoices billed to an Account.

All e2e test rows and Stripe test objects were cleaned up afterwards.
