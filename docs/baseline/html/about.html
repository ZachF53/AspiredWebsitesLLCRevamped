Why This Matters

Most Web Designers Don’t Think About Security. I Do.

Your Website Is a Front Door

Every contact form, every intake submission, every visitor interaction is a potential security exposure. I build with that in mind, not as an afterthought.

Your Customers Trust You With Their Home

Booking forms and review requests both handle real customer contact information. That data deserves the same security discipline as the HVAC work itself.

Monthly Security Audits: Included

Every maintenance plan includes a monthly security scan, summarized in a one-page report emailed to you on the 1st. Not an upsell. Standard.

Credentials

Verify Any Of This Yourself

Anyone can put letters after their name on a website. Here’s where to check.

CISSP: (ISC)²

Certified Information Systems Security Professional. It requires five years of paid security experience across multiple domains, a supervised exam, an endorsement from an existing member, and ongoing continuing education to keep.

Verify a certification with (ISC)² →

M.S. Cybersecurity

A master’s degree in cybersecurity, the academic side of the same discipline: cryptography, secure architecture, risk, and incident response.

Degree verification is available through the issuing institution on request.

What this actually means for you: security decisions get made during the build rather than bolted on afterwards. It does not mean your rankings improve because of it. That’s not how search works, and we won’t pretend otherwise.

Fit

Who This Is For (And Who It Isn’t)

We’re A Good Fit If

  • You’re an HVAC contractor where one job is worth more than the build
  • You want to own the site outright rather than rent it
  • Your website has a job to do: intake, bookings, being found
  • You’d rather hear “you don’t need that” than be upsold
  • You value being able to reach the person who actually built it

We’re Probably Not If

  • You need a single page with your hours: a $16/month builder is genuinely better value
  • You need it live next week; a proper build takes three to four weeks
  • You want guaranteed rankings: nobody can honestly promise those
  • You want the cheapest possible option; we’re not it
  • You need a large in-house team on retainer: that’s an agency, not us
