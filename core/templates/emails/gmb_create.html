Hi {{ name }} — let’s get you on Google Maps.

First create your Google Business Profile, then add us as a Manager so we can run it for you.

Part 1 — Create your profile

  1. On a computer, sign in to your Google account (or create a free one at accounts.google.com).
  2. Go to business.google.com/add and click “Add your business to Google”.
  3. Enter your business name.
  4. Choose your business category, then click Next.
  5. Choose whether customers can visit you: “Yes” (add your address / place the map marker) or “No” (set the areas you serve).
  6. Add your phone number and website, then Continue.
  7. Pick a verification method (postcard code, phone, email, or video) and follow the steps. Verification can take a few days; your profile shows on Google once verified.

Part 2 — Add us as a Manager

  1. In your Business Profile, open Menu / “More” → “Business Profile settings” → “People and access”.
  2. Click “Add” and enter our email: {{ manager_email }}
  3. Under “Access” choose “Manager”, then click “Invite”.

We’ll accept the invite and handle the rest. Reply to this email any time and we’ll walk you through it.
